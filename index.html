ÉLAN NOIR is built around contrast. It is both clean and dark, delicate and powerful, distant and deeply personal. The fragrance reflects a modern interpretation of luxury — one that avoids excess and instead focuses on precision, material, atmosphere and emotion. Every detail, from the composition itself to the bottle and presentation, is designed
to feel intentional.

At its core is about memory. The scent is meant to become associated with a place, a person, a certain hour of the evening, or a moment that cannot be repeated. It creates a subtle trace that remains after movement has stopped, after conversation has ended, after the room has gone quiet. In this way fragrance becomes more than an accessory.

Created for those who appreciate restraint, detail and atmosphere, belongs to a world where luxury is felt rather than displayed. It is a fragrance for late evenings, quiet interiors, soft light and close distance. A composition that does not demand attention, but naturally holds it. A scent that becomes more intimate the longer it stays on the skin.
